## Selected work

**[ZenFit](https://github.com/2deva/ZenFit)**
A voice fitness coach on Gemini Live. It notices a broken streak, offers a short restart instead of a full workout, and talks you through it so you never have to pick up the phone. The agent is evaluated, not just prompted. Sessions are traced in Opik against whether the person actually comes back, whether the next step is concrete, and whether the model stays in its lane.

**[MarketAI](https://github.com/2deva/MarketAI)**
Marketing images from a short brief. Flask and Tailwind in front of DALL·E, with a style, a size, and a download, rather than a raw API call left in a notebook.

**[Phishy](https://github.com/2deva/Phishy)**
A machine-learning check for phishing sites. The point is a decision you can act on, not a leaderboard score.

**[Line-Follower](https://github.com/2deva/Line-Follower)**
A Raspberry Pi car that follows a line, driven from a Python and Flask remote. Small hardware, a real control loop.

**[Delivery drone](https://github.com/2deva/Capstone-Project-Delivery-Drone-)**
A low-cost autonomous drone built to carry a light package a short distance and release it on a marked drop zone.
